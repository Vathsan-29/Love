# Azhagiii — From Vathzzz 🤍

A romantic, cute, playful and emotional single-page website made with:
- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

## How to open
1. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder.
2. Double-click `index.html`.
3. It will open in your browser.

No server, database, framework, or installation is required.

## Personalization
All visible words are in `index.html`.
The design is in `style.css`.
Interactions/animations are in `script.js`.

The website intentionally avoids claiming a long shared history because the relationship is just beginning. It focuses on genuine feelings, getting to know each other, her being herself, and giving the future room to unfold naturally.
